## 1. Categories and sources

We receive directly from you your weight, height, sex entered for the formula, age, activity level, body goal, foods, portions and water consumed, and optional progress photos.

With your consent, we generate estimates of calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat and fibre. Your workout history, completed days, loads and reps are part of the training service and are covered by the general Privacy Policy. A photograph may reveal physical characteristics.

We do not request medical records, diagnoses, medications, pregnancy information, identification biometrics, or wearable-device data for these features.

If you voluntarily post health information in BuildFam, other members may see it; we ask that you avoid doing so.

## 2. Why we collect and use this information

These categories are used to calculate general nutritional estimates, display routines and progress, save your logs, and allow you to compare your own photographs.

We limit access to what is necessary to provide the requested features, offer support, maintain security, and comply with the law. Automated calculations are estimates for informational purposes and do not constitute medical care.

Certain data may be required for a specific feature; progress photos are always optional.

## 3. Consent and control

During registration, we provide a health data consent checkbox separate from acceptance of the Terms, which is unchecked by default. The [Health and Fitness Data Consent](https://mariagabsfitness.com/pages/health-data-consent-en) identifies the categories, purposes, recipients, and withdrawal process.

To create an account, we ask you to tick that box. If you change your mind later, you can withdraw it with one tap; your account and workouts stay active.

We keep a record of your choice and the version of the information presented. Consent does not authorize advertising, promotional campaigns involving images, or public sharing of your data.

You can view and withdraw your consent in Profile → Legal & privacy → Manage consents, with one tap and one confirmation. You can also email team@mariagabsfitness.com. When you withdraw it, we stop all processing based on it, including on our servers. We turn off SmartFuel, water tracking and progress photos, and delete your plan, meals, water log and progress photos. We keep only a record of the withdrawal (date, version and consent type), with no health data, so we can demonstrate it. Your account and subscription stay active. You can give consent again from the same screen.

Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of previous processing; any subsequent retention requires a separate and limited legal justification. You do not need to repeat a valid consent for the same purpose unless material changes require it.

Private progress photos are optional and are uploaded only when you choose to do so, with prior information about their purpose and visibility.

When uploading a photo, there may be an optional checkbox allowing us to contact you to request permission to use it. Checking this box does not authorize publication, advertising, or disclosure.

Any promotional use requires subsequent, specific, freely given, and documented authorization describing the photograph, channels, duration, and withdrawal rights where applicable.

## 4. Recipients and disclosures

Google Firebase (Firestore and Storage) hosts account data, progress information, and images and provides technical services under BuildUp’s instructions. Authorized personnel and support providers may access only what is strictly necessary.

We do not share health data with Vimeo to personalize videos, or with RevenueCat, Apple, or Google to build advertising audiences. The device or video provider may receive technical playback information.

Voluntary posts in BuildFam are shared with other members; we do not automatically include your private nutrition or progress data in posts.

We may disclose data if required by law or when necessary to respond to a serious threat, in accordance with applicable regulations.

We do not sell consumer health data.

We do not use geofencing around healthcare facilities or advertising based on health information.

If we ever propose a new category, recipient, or purpose, we will update this page and obtain any consent or authorization required by law beforehand.

## 5. Rights, requests, and appeals

Depending on applicable law, you may confirm whether we collect or share your health data, access that data and learn about the categories of sources and recipients, withdraw consent, request deletion, and appeal a denial.

Email team@mariagabsfitness.com with the subject “BuildUp Health Data,” or use our [account deletion page](https://mariagabsfitness.com/pages/delete-account-en).

We may request limited information to verify that you are the account holder.

To appeal, reply to the same email with “Health Privacy Appeal”; we will inform you of our decision and the authority you may contact where applicable.

We do not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

## 6. Retention, protection, and changes

Private tracking data and photographs are retained while you maintain your account and need them for the relevant feature, unless deleted earlier.

Following a verified request, they are deleted from active systems within applicable legal deadlines, subject to limited legal exceptions; residual backups follow the cycle described in the general Privacy Policy and are not used to provide the service.

Access to this data is restricted to authorized personnel and providers subject to security controls.

Health data belonging to EU residents is also subject to the rights and legal bases described in the [general Privacy Policy](https://mariagabsfitness.com/pages/privacy-en).

If we make material changes, we will notify you before expanding the categories or purposes.
