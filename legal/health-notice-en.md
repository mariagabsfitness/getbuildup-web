Calorie and nutrient targets are calculated using general formulas based on the information you enter and are not individually reviewed by a professional.

Food and recipe values are approximate. They are not intended to manage illnesses, allergies, deficiencies, or medical needs.

Results vary from person to person, and we do not guarantee weight loss, muscle gain, or specific transformations.

## When to consult a professional and stop

Consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting or changing an exercise or nutrition routine, especially if you have an illness, injury, heart or metabolic condition, take medication, are pregnant, postpartum, or breastfeeding, or have a history of eating disorders.

If calorie counting causes distress or risky behavior, stop using that feature and seek professional guidance.

Exercise carries a risk of injury. Use equipment in good condition, a safe space, and technique and loads appropriate to your ability.

Stop if you experience pain, dizziness, unusual breathing difficulties, or other concerning symptoms.

**In an emergency, seek urgent care; neither the app nor BuildFam is an emergency service.**
