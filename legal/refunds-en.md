# Refund Policy

**BuildUp · MARIA GABS FITNESS LLC**

**Last updated: November 2, 2026**

BuildUp is a digital subscription service from MARIA GABS FITNESS LLC. This policy explains how cancellations and refunds work depending on where you purchased your membership.

## 7-day free trial

Every new membership includes 7 days free. If you cancel before the trial ends, you are not charged anything. We recommend cancelling at least 24 hours before the end of the trial period.

## If you purchased on our website

Payments are processed through Paddle.com, which acts as the merchant of record for our digital products. You may cancel your subscription at any time; cancelling stops future renewals and you keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

If you request a refund within 14 days of a charge and you have not used the service substantially, we process it without asking questions. After that period, we review each case individually. Write to us at [team@mariagabsfitness.com](mailto:team@mariagabsfitness.com) with your account email and, if you have it, your Paddle order number. We respond within 5 business days. Approved refunds are returned to the same payment method and usually take between 5 and 10 business days.

If you reside in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you also have the statutory 14-day right of withdrawal. By starting immediate access to the digital content, you accept that this right may be extinguished once performance of the service has begun.

## If you purchased in the app on an iPhone or iPad

The purchase is handled by Apple. Cancellations and refunds are requested in your Apple account settings or at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue those refunds.

## If you purchased in the app on Android

The purchase is handled by Google Play. Cancellations and refunds are requested from your Google Play account. We cannot issue those refunds.
