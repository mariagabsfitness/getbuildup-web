# Delete Your BuildUp Account and Data

**BuildUp · MARIA GABS FITNESS LLC**

## Request deletion of your account and data

If you have access to BuildUp, go to **Profile → Delete my account** and follow the verification and confirmation instructions.

If you cannot access the app, email [team@mariagabsfitness.com](mailto:team@mariagabsfitness.com?subject=Delete%20BuildUp%20account) from the email address associated with your account, using the subject **“Delete BuildUp account.”**

State the account’s email address and that you request deletion; **do not send your password, photos, or health data in the message.**

This web-based option lets you start the request without installing the app.

## What happens to your account and data

We will verify that the request comes from the account holder. Once confirmed, we will delete the account and associated active data, including progress, meals, photos, and linked content, subject to the limited legal exceptions explained in the [Privacy Policy](https://mariagabsfitness.com/pages/privacy-en) and the [Health Data Privacy Policy](https://mariagabsfitness.com/pages/health-data-privacy-en).

Residual copies in technical backups are deleted according to the rotation cycle and retention applicable to the service and its configuration, for limited technical recovery purposes.

They are not used for marketing or returned to ordinary use after a deletion request; if recovery restores them, we reapply deletion.

Applicable legal deadlines are respected. We will inform you of the status of your request and comply with the deadlines imposed by your jurisdiction.

## Subscription cancellation

**Important: deleting BuildUp does not cancel charges managed by Apple or Google.**

Cancel your subscription separately in the subscription settings of your Apple or Google account before deleting your account if you do not wish to renew.
