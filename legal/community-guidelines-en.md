# BuildFam Community Guidelines

**BuildUp · MARIA GABS FITNESS LLC**

**Last updated: October 15, 2026**

These guidelines supplement the [Terms of Service](https://mariagabsfitness.com/pages/terms-en) and must be accepted before posting. BuildFam is a space for adults of any gender.

## Respect and safety

**Zero tolerance.** BuildFam does not tolerate abusive content, harassment, threats, hate, discrimination, exploitation, sexually explicit content, or illegal material. These prohibitions also form part of the Terms accepted at registration.

Moderation measures depend on severity, risk, and repeated violations and include removing content, restricting features, and suspending or closing accounts, subject to applicable legal safeguards.

Do not post threats, harassment, intimidation, hate, discrimination, exploitation, sexually explicit content, self-harm instructions, or illegal content.

Do not attack another person’s body, weight, gender, origin, or health status. Do not promote extreme diets, purging, non-indicated medication, or dangerous workouts, or claim to diagnose or treat a condition.

In the event of immediate danger, contact emergency services; BuildFam is not a crisis service.

## Privacy and authenticity

Only share images and content you have the right to publish. Do not disclose other people’s private information, photos, or health data without their permission.

Posts may be visible to other members and are not an appropriate place to store private progress photos.

Do not impersonate others, send spam, sell products without authorization, or manipulate interactions.

## Reporting, blocking, and moderation

Use **“Report”** on a post or profile to flag a violation and **“Block”** to stop interacting with another person.

You may also email [buildup@mariagabsfitness.com](mailto:buildup@mariagabsfitness.com), describing the content and risk without sending more health data than necessary.

We review reports and prioritize urgent risks; we may hide, remove, or limit content, issue warnings, or suspend or close accounts depending on severity and repeated violations.

A report does not guarantee that we will remove lawful content that complies with the guidelines. If you believe there has been an error, request a review at the same email address.

The community has filtering, reporting, blocking, and moderation mechanisms; if you believe they are not working, let us know at the contact email.

## Acceptance

By checking **“I accept the BuildFam Community Guidelines”** before your first post, you agree to comply with them.

If they change materially, we will inform you and request renewed acceptance where appropriate.
