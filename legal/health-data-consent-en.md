What data and for what purposes

If you choose to use SmartFuel, water tracking or progress photos, we process the data you give us about your weight, height, age, sex entered for the formula, activity level and goal; your meals, portions and water; and your progress photos.

From these data, we may generate calorie and macronutrient estimates, history, and consistency metrics.

The purpose is to provide the fitness and nutrition tracking tools you request, save your progress, and show general estimates, not to diagnose or treat conditions. We only request the data needed for the feature you choose.

Private progress photos are optional and are uploaded only when you choose, with information visible when you upload them.

Recipients and limits

Google Firebase hosts these records and optional images through Firestore and Storage. Authorized personnel and service providers may access only what is strictly necessary to operate, protect, and support the features.

Processing may take place in the United States and other provider locations, subject to the safeguards explained in the [Privacy Policy](https://mariagabsfitness.com/pages/privacy-en).

We do not sell these data or use them for targeted advertising or advertising audiences.

This checkbox does not authorize publishing photos, sharing data in BuildFam, sending you promotions, or using your image in campaigns.

Your choice and how to withdraw it

Ticking this box is required to create your account. You can withdraw it whenever you want without losing your account or workouts.

To withdraw it, go to Profile → Legal & privacy → Manage consents and tap “Withdraw consent”. Withdrawal takes effect immediately. You can also email team@mariagabsfitness.com. When you withdraw it, SmartFuel, water tracking and progress photos turn off and we delete that data. Your workouts, account and subscription stay the same; your subscription is not cancelled automatically.

You may also request deletion of data already collected. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of prior processing, and any subsequent retention requires a separate, limited legal justification.

See the [Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy](https://mariagabsfitness.com/pages/health-data-privacy-en) for full categories, retention, recipients, and rights.

We keep a record of your choice, its date, and the version of the information presented. Requesting access to general app information does not authorize recording health data without your consent.
