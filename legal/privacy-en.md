## 2. Information we process

- Account and identity: name, email address, date of birth, user identifier, registration date, credentials managed by Firebase Authentication, optional profile photo, and support communications. We do not receive your password in plain text.
- Training: selected program and variation, calendar and completed days, sets, repetitions, loads, unit preferences, personal records, consistency, ratings, and progress. We process this data to provide the training service you signed up for; it does not depend on your consent for health data.
- Nutrition and fitness: sex entered for the calculation, age, weight, height, activity level, goal, calorie and macronutrient estimates, logged foods and quantities, recipes viewed, and water consumption. Some of these categories may reveal health data.
- Images: progress photos and the profile photo you choose to upload; your voluntary choice of whether we may contact you to request specific permission to use an image. Selecting this option does not cause your progress photos to be published.
- BuildFam: display name, posts, comments, shared images, reports, and moderation actions. Your posts may be visible to other members according to the community settings; avoid including sensitive information about yourself or others.
- Subscription: purchase identifiers, product, status, duration, and subscription events supplied by Apple or Google and managed through RevenueCat. Apple or Google processes the payment. We do not receive your complete card number.
- Technical data: installation and device identifiers, app version, model, operating system, IP address or approximate country derived from it, errors, timings and performance metrics, and security logs needed to operate and protect the service. Firebase Performance Monitoring is enabled.
- Notifications: notification token, preferences, and data needed to deliver notifications if you grant permission on your device.

The camera and photo library are used when you choose to select or take an image. An achievement image you share through your phone’s sharing menu is under your control and that of the service you select.

We do not integrate contacts, microphone access, precise geolocation, HealthKit, or Health Connect in the version described here. We do not use Open Food Facts in this version.

## 3. Purposes and legal bases

- Providing the service and performing our contract with you: creating and protecting your account and enabling routines, nutrition, tracking, videos, community, subscriptions, and support.
- Consent: processing health and fitness data where required by law, storing optional photos, sending promotional communications when consent is required, and asking for separate permission before using your image publicly.

You can withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it, in the app under Profile → Legal & privacy → Manage consents. Withdrawal takes effect immediately; you do not need to email us. You can also email team@mariagabsfitness.com. When you withdraw consent for health and fitness data, we stop processing it, turn off SmartFuel, water tracking and progress photos, and delete that data. Your account, your workouts and your subscription are not affected; you manage your subscription separately in Profile → Membership or in your app store settings. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before it.

- Legitimate interests, subject to your rights: preventing abuse and fraud, moderating BuildFam, resolving technical problems, defending legal claims, and improving app stability without using your health data for targeted advertising.
- Legal obligations: handling rights requests, keeping legally required records, and responding to valid requests from authorities.

If you are in the European Economic Area, explicit consent for special categories of data, where applicable, is separate from acceptance of the Terms. You must also be able to decline promotional communications separately.

We do not make solely automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects; calorie and macro calculations are automated estimates for informational use.

Sign-up asks for two separate boxes: acceptance of the Terms and consent for health and fitness data. You can withdraw that consent at any time in Profile → Legal & privacy → Manage consents and keep using your account and workouts.

Marketing permissions and permission to use images for promotion are managed separately.

## 4. Who we share data with

We use providers subject to the applicable instructions and safeguards: Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Storage, and Performance Monitoring) for accounts, storage, security, and diagnostics; RevenueCat for subscription status; Apple and Google for distribution, purchases, and notifications depending on the platform; and Vimeo for playing exercise videos.

Playback may communicate technical data, such as your IP address and device information, to Vimeo; please also review its policy.

We may give authorized personnel and contractors limited access for support, security, and moderation. Content you post in BuildFam is shared with other members.

We may disclose information to comply with a legal obligation or protect rights and safety, subject to applicable safeguards.

We do not sell personal information or health data; we do not include advertising in the app or send weight, meals, goals, workouts, or photos to advertising platforms to create audiences.

We do not use photos in campaigns without specific, documented permission identifying the image, media, and scope.

## 5. Notifications and messages

With your device permission, we may send workout, nutrition, and water reminders, progress notifications, birthday messages, BuildFam activity notices, and promotional communications.

BuildUp does not currently send promotional emails or push notifications. If we start, we will ask for separate permission for each channel, which you can withdraw in Profile → Legal & privacy → Manage consents and, in emails, through the unsubscribe link. Your phone’s notification permission is different: it only lets the system show alerts, does not authorise promotions, and is changed in your phone settings.

Operational notices needed for your account may continue while you keep it. You can change push notification permission in your phone’s settings.

## 6. Retention and deletion

We retain account and progress data while your account is active or until you request deletion.

When we verify and process deletion, we delete active data associated with the account, including photos and identifiable posts, except specific data we must retain for legal obligations, security, disputes, or the defense of claims for as long as necessary.

Residual copies in technical backups are deleted according to the rotation cycle and retention periods applicable to the service and its configuration. Retention is limited to technical recovery needs; these copies are not used for marketing or returned to ordinary service use after a deletion request.

If technical recovery restores data subject to a deletion request, we reapply that request. We comply with deletion deadlines and obligations under applicable law.

Transaction records subject to tax obligations are retained for the applicable statutory periods. We may retain truly anonymized data that no longer identify you.

Deleting your BuildUp account does not automatically cancel a subscription billed by Apple or Google.

Request deletion through the app or at [mariagabsfitness.com/pages/delete-account-en](https://mariagabsfitness.com/pages/delete-account-en). Contact us if you need to delete only specific data. Before acting, we may verify your identity using proportionate measures.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you reside, you may request information, access, correction, a copy or portability, objection, restriction, and deletion; you may also withdraw consent without affecting prior processing and complain to the relevant data protection authority.

In US jurisdictions with additional rights, including those relating to health data in Washington and Nevada, you may request confirmation of processing and access to categories and recipients, and appeal where applicable.

Email team@mariagabsfitness.com with the subject “BuildUp Privacy”; if we deny a request, you can reply to the same email with “Appeal.”

We respond within the deadlines set by applicable law. We do not discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights.

## 8. International transfers and security

We are a company based in the United States; our providers may process information outside your country of residence, including in the United States.

For transfers subject to the GDPR, we will use the applicable mechanism, such as an adequacy decision valid for the relevant provider or standard contractual clauses together with supplementary measures where necessary.

We apply access controls, authentication, permission restrictions, and proportionate technical and organizational measures. No system is infallible. We will assess and notify incidents in accordance with applicable laws.

## 9. Age, changes, and links

We may update this policy; we will publish a new date and, where the change requires it, request new consent before processing data for different purposes.
